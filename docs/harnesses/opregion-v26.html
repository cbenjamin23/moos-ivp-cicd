Behavior Correctness

H02 OpRegion V26

Behavior correctness tests for BHV_OpRegionV26. These cases isolate generalized-polygon parsing, active contained-state keep-in, lapsed recovery, entry gating, runtime region replacement, disabled keep-in, metric distance output, and delayed breach flags.

Stem Mission

Baseline scenario context

The harness copies the proven OpRegion motion mission, replaces its behavior stem with one BHV_OpRegionV26 instance, and applies isolated case overlays for generalized borders, convex covers, recovery geometry, runtime updates, and mission-owned regression evidence.

Examples

Representative runs

Convex Keep-In

Description: runs a contained transit in a single-cover convex region and requires an active V26 execution signal with no lapse or breach.

Non-Convex Keep-In

Description: runs the same contained transit through an L-shaped generalized border represented by two convex cover polygons.

Lapsed Recovery

Description: temporarily shrinks the active border around a moving vehicle without reaching the breach distance, then restores the region and requires recovery evidence plus route completion.

Case Matrix

All cases

convex_contained_keepin_pass

runs a contained transit in a single-cover convex region and requires an active V26 execution signal with no lapse or breach.

nonconvex_contained_keepin_pass

runs the same contained transit through an L-shaped generalized border represented by two convex cover polygons.

lapsed_recovery_pass

temporarily shrinks the active border around a moving vehicle without reaching the breach distance, then restores the region and requires recovery evidence plus route completion.

entry_gate_start_outside_pass

starts outside the region with entry gating enabled and requires the waypoint behavior to carry the vehicle inside before containment becomes active.

dynamic_region_update_pass

replaces the generalized polygon at runtime and requires the resulting lapsed/recovery transition without a terminal breach.

stay_in_disabled_breach_pass

disables the new contained-state keep-in objective and passes when the expected terminal breach and helm error are observed.

metric_distance_macro_pass

requires a real distance between 4 and 14 meters in the controlled short transit, rejecting the former boolean 1.

delayed_breach_flag_pass

requires both the configured breach flag and helm error after the delay expires.
